## Key Provisions & Material Scopes

### Section 41: Adulteration and Prohibited Sale
- **Summary:** Prohibits selling or offering to sell goods adulterated with harmful ingredients or noxious materials.
- **Application:** Substandard, toxic, expired, or chemically contaminated food and consumer goods.

### Section 44: Deceptive Advertising and Misrepresentation
- **Summary:** Penalizes false or misleading advertisements designed to deceive consumers regarding the quality, quantity, purity, or standard of goods or services.
- **Application:** E-commerce misrepresentation, misleading promotions, and bait-and-switch listings.

### Section 45: Failure to Sell/Deliver Promised Goods or Services
- **Summary:** Mandates that once money or consideration is accepted, the seller must deliver the agreed goods or perform the promised service without unjustified refusal or default.
- **Application:** E-commerce delivery default, failure to supply ordered items, and undelivered advance-paid products.

### Section 60: Limitation Period for Complaints
- **Summary:** Consumer complaints to the Director General or designated officer must be submitted within **30 days** from the cause of action (the date of occurrence or discovery of the offense).
- **Material Constraint:** Timelines are strictly enforced; complaints filed after 30 days without statutory cause are subject to procedural dismissal.

### Section 76: Complainant Reward (25%)
- **Summary:** When an administrative fine is levied and realized against a violating merchant/establishment, **25 percent** of the collected penalty is disbursed to the aggrieved consumer complainant.
- **Application:** Incentivizes formal consumer participation; explained honestly in guidance without guaranteeing monetary recovery.
