## Procedural Workflow & Requirements

### 1. Account & Identity Verification
- Complainant must register using a verified Bangladesh National ID (NID) and active mobile phone number.
- Accurate consumer contact details and home address are required for hearing summons.

### 2. Mandatory Evidence Attachments
- **Invoice / Receipt:** Cash memo, e-commerce order confirmation, or tax invoice showing merchant name, BIN/trade license (if available), and date.
- **Proof of Payment:** Bank transfer slip, MFS transaction ID (TrxID) screenshot, or cash receipt.
- **Defect / Breach Documentation:** Clear unboxing video/photograph for defective items; screenshot of tracking status showing unfulfilled delivery.
- **Prior Notice / Merchant Communication:** Screenshots of support chats, SMS, emails, or call logs demonstrating merchant refusal or non-responsiveness.

### 3. Submission & Tracking
- Submissions generate a unique CCMS Tracking Number.
- Complainants receive SMS alerts for hearing dates, mediation sessions, or administrative orders.
- Both parties are summoned to the relevant district/divisional DNCRP office for reconciliation before formal adjudication.
