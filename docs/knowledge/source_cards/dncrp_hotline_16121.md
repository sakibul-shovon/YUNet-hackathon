## Operating Parameters & Scope

### 1. Hours & Accessibility
- Available across all mobile and landline telecom operators in Bangladesh at short-code **16121**.
- Live agent hours: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM on Bangladesh government working days (Sunday through Thursday).
- Automated interactive voice recording for after-hours reporting.

### 2. Immediate Incident Intake
- Priority cases:
  - Acute food safety risks (chemical contamination, toxic adulteration).
  - Hoarding and sudden artificial price inflation of essential commodities.
  - Dangerous/hazardous consumer appliances or counterfeit medicines.
- Dispatch capability: Hotline operators can alert local mobile courts and district executive magistrates for immediate physical inspection and enforcement.

### 3. Limitation
- Calling 16121 provides immediate alert dispatch, but does **not** substitute for the formal documentary filing under Section 60 of CRPA 2009 required for claiming the 25% statutory reward or permanent administrative restitution.
