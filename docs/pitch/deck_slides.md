# Ebarki - five-slide submission content

Content source, updated 5 November 2026. This Markdown file is not an exported presentation deck. Verify the final deck and live deployment before submission.

## Slide 1 - An everyday consumer problem

The receipt is in a gallery. A delivery promise is in a message. A consumer needs to understand what happened and prepare a clear next step. Ebarki brings those pieces into a private, reviewable workspace for Bangladesh users.

## Slide 2 - Follow the evidence

Tell the story in English, Bangla or Banglish. Add genuine images, PDFs or audio. Inspect the original beside proposed findings. Confirm what is right, correct dates and leave unrelated records out. Two explicitly reviewed payments can resolve a discrepancy without changing the original account.

## Slide 3 - Meaningful AI with inspectable boundaries

Configured Groq models provide situation summaries, image reading, speech transcription, contextual explanations and buyer-to-seller reply wording after case consent. Provider acknowledgements distinguish fresh calls from cached readings. Local PDF extraction, lexical retrieval and procedural rules are identified separately. AI outputs need review; they are not legal determinations.

## Slide 4 - A useful next action

Explore procedural conditions and what-if changes. Prepare and edit a reply or a source-linked document. Download a reviewed PDF or evidence archive. Copy prepared fields and complete any official steps personally. Ebarki does not send messages, file complaints or guarantee outcomes.
