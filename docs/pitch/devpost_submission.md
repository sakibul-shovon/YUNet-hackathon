**Presentation:** [Five-slide content](deck_slides.md); an actual exported submission deck still needs verification.

Consumers often have a story, payment receipts and seller messages in separate places. Ebarki helps organize those records, review important details, compare payments and prepare an editable next action.

English and Bangla interfaces share a private case workspace. Real configured Groq models provide bounded summaries, image reading, speech transcription, explanations and reply wording after consent. Native PDFs use local extraction. Lexical source retrieval and three-valued procedural rules remain separate from model wording. Provider acknowledgements and source links make execution inspectable. Important claims require human review.

The independent audit exercised fictional new cases through the real web BFF, plus a fresh native-Chrome consumer flow. See the [system audit](../qa/EBARKI_FINAL_SYSTEM_AUDIT.md), [feature matrix](../qa/EBARKI_FEATURE_TEST_MATRIX.md) and [issue ledger](../qa/EBARKI_ISSUE_LEDGER.md) for exact coverage and limitations.

Ebarki prepares material; it does not contact sellers, send notices, submit government complaints, establish liability, authenticate evidence or guarantee refunds. Public merchant data is empty and community publishing is unavailable pending a reviewed dataset, durable storage and moderation. Scanned PDFs require manual transcription. Speech and image readings require checking against the original.

Avoid historical claims about automatic dispatch, wallet freezes, legal ultimatums, class actions, perfect AI accuracy, universal accessibility compliance or production readiness. They are not supported by this audit.

Competition requirements are on the [official rules page](https://yunet-aignite26.devpost.com/rules). That page currently displays conflicting deadline times between its header and rules body; do not silently resolve the conflict. Eligibility, sticker provenance, the hosted URL and submitted deck must be checked before final submission.
