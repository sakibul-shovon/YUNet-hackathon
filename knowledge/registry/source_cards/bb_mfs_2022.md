## Consumer Protection & Dispute Standards

### 1. Unauthorized Transaction Reporting
- Account holders must report unauthorized deductions, fraudulent transfers, or system double-charges promptly.
- Reporting window: Within **30 calendar days** from the transaction timestamp for expedited review.

### 2. Provider Investigation Burden & Timeline
- Upon receiving a dispute notification, the MFS provider must log an immutable audit entry and provide the consumer a dispute reference code.
- Initial investigative response must be rendered within **7 working days**.
- If technical error is confirmed, chargeback/reversal must occur without undue delay.

### 3. Exclusions & Consumer Responsibility
- Voluntary disclosure of Secret PIN or OTP to third parties (social engineering/phishing where customer validated authentication) generally places burden on customer unless systemic provider security breach is proven.
- Systemic failure (e.g., duplicate debits, wallet synchronization bugs) remains the strict liability of the MFS operator.
